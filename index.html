# Privacy Policy for Walkscreen **Last updated: August 25, 2026** Walkscreen is designed with privacy in mind. Your personal data stays on your device, and Walkscreen does not operate servers that collect or store your personal information. ## Data Collection Walkscreen does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with third parties. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics SDKs. ## Screen Time Walkscreen uses Apple's Screen Time APIs to provide its core app-blocking and screen-time management features. Screen Time information is accessed through Apple's system frameworks and is used only to provide these features. Walkscreen does not transmit your Screen Time information to the developer or any external server. ## Location Walkscreen may request access to your location to provide location-based features, such as discovering or verifying visits to landmarks and places. Location information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or stored on external servers. ## Camera and Photos Walkscreen may request access to the camera or photo library for features that involve taking or selecting photos. Photos and related information used by these features are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to Walkscreen servers. ## Data Storage Information created or used by Walkscreen may be stored locally on your device as necessary for the app to function. Walkscreen does not operate a remote user database or cloud backend for storing your personal information. Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data, subject to Apple's iOS behavior and system-managed data. ## Third-Party Services Walkscreen relies on Apple-provided iOS frameworks and system services to provide certain functionality. Walkscreen does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services that collect your personal information. ## Children's Privacy Walkscreen does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because personal information is not collected by the developer, Walkscreen does not maintain a database of children's personal information. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if Walkscreen's features or data practices change.
