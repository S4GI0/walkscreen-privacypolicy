Walkscreen Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

Walkscreen is designed with privacy in mind. The app processes the information required for its features on your device whenever possible.

Data Collection

Walkscreen does not require you to create an account.

Walkscreen does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs to track you across apps or websites.

Location

Walkscreen uses location information to provide location-based features, such as map exploration, nearby challenges, and progress within the app.

Location information used by Walkscreen is processed for the app's functionality and is not sold to third parties.

Photos and Camera

Walkscreen may request access to the camera or photos when you choose to use features involving challenges and memories. Photos used for these features are processed as part of the app experience.

Screen Time

Walkscreen uses Apple's Screen Time APIs to provide features that allow you to manage selected apps and use Screen Time earned through Walkscreen.

Screen Time controls are handled using Apple's system frameworks. Walkscreen does not use this functionality for advertising or tracking.

Walkscreen+

Walkscreen offers an optional paid subscription called Walkscreen+. Purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

Walkscreen does not receive or store your payment card information.

Third-Party Services

Walkscreen relies on Apple system services and frameworks where necessary to provide its functionality. Apple may process information according to its own privacy policies when you use services provided by Apple.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Walkscreen evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Walkscreen's privacy practices, contact:

contact@walkscreen.app

Support

For help with Walkscreen, visit the Walkscreen Support page.
