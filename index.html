Privacy Policy for Walkscreen

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Walkscreen is designed with privacy in mind. Your personal data stays on your device, and Walkscreen does not operate servers that collect or store your personal information.

Data Collection

Walkscreen does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with third parties.

Screen Time

Walkscreen uses Apple's Screen Time APIs to provide its core app-blocking and screen-time management features.

Location

Walkscreen may request access to your location for location-based features. Location information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Camera and Photos

Walkscreen may request access to the camera or photo library for features involving taking or selecting photos. These are processed locally.
